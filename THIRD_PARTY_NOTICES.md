PulseGrid bundles `src/MidiEditor/Assets/SoundFonts/ChaosBank.sf2` as the default General MIDI SoundFont.

- Catalog/source mirror: https://github.com/bratpeki/soundfonts
- Original bank source: https://rkhive.com/banks.html

The SoundFont is distributed separately from PulseGrid's source code under its own CC0/public-domain dedication.

## OpenUtau interoperability

PulseGrid can export a Vocal track as UST and launch a separately installed OpenUtau executable. OpenUtau binaries or source code are **not** bundled with PulseGrid.

- OpenUtau project: https://github.com/openutau/OpenUtau
- OpenUtau license: MIT

## PulseGrid Default Voice

The files under `src/MidiEditor/Assets/Voicebanks/PulseGridDefault` were generated specifically for PulseGrid. They are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0** so that a clean installation always has a small OpenUtau/UTAU-compatible test voicebank.
